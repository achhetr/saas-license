## 2. Information Collection and Use
We believe that your data should stay yours. 

* **No Personal Data Collection:** Top5Percent does **not** collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.
* **Local Storage Only:** All gameplay data—including reaction times, high scores, and your chosen display name—is stored **locally on your device**. 
* **No Server Transmission:** This data is never transmitted to an external server, database, or third party. If you uninstall the app, your local gameplay data will be deleted.

---

## 3. Analytics
* **Local Statistics:** We use internal logic to calculate your performance statistics for gameplay purposes only. 
* **No Third-Party Analytics:** In the current version, we do not use external analytics services (such as Firebase Analytics or Google Analytics) that track user behavior across sessions.

---

## 4. Advertising
* **Current Status:** Top5Percent is currently **ad-free**. 
* **Future Updates:** Should future versions include advertisements, this policy will be updated to disclose the use of advertising identifiers and any data shared with ad networks.

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy
Our app is intended for a general audience. Because we **do not collect any personal information**, Top5Percent is compliant with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect or solicit any information from children under the age of 13.

---

## 6. Security
While we do not transmit data over the internet, we encourage users to secure their devices with standard biometric or passcode locks to protect the local data stored on the hardware.
