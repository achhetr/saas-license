# Privacy Policy for AkashBro Calorie

**Last updated and effective date:** 23 October 2026

**Developer:** Akash Chhetri (akashbro)  
**Contact:** android.curler416@passfwd.com  
**Android package:** `com.akashbro.calorie`

This policy explains how AkashBro Calorie (also referred to as akashbro-calories) handles information. The app provides food and calorie logging, nutrition targets, weight tracking, water tracking, fasting tracking and optional reminders. No account is required.

## Information stored and used by the app

Information you enter is stored in a local SQLite database on your device. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

- Optional profile information, such as age, sex, height, weight and activity level.
- Calorie goals, macro and fibre targets, and inputs used for nutrition estimates.
- Food diary entries, quantities, meal dates, nutrition values, custom meals, favourites and remembered food quantities.
- Weight readings, water goals and drink records, serving sizes, and fasting schedules and sessions.
- Tracking preferences, reminder settings and notification scheduling information.

The app uses this information to display your records, calculate nutrition estimates and summaries, manage your goals and schedule reminders. Health and fitness information is used for these app features, not for advertising or profiling by the developer.

## Collection, sharing and tracking

The app does not send your profile, health information or diary records to the developer or a developer-operated server. There are no accounts, developer-operated cloud sync services, advertisements, advertising identifiers, third-party analytics or third-party crash-reporting services in the current app. We do not sell or share your app records with advertisers or data brokers.

The food catalogue is bundled with the app and can be searched offline. The app does not connect to Health Connect or read health records from other apps.

## Device backups and platform services

Local storage does not mean that your device can never copy the data elsewhere. Android backup is currently enabled. Depending on your operating system, device manufacturer and backup settings, app data may be included in system-managed cloud backups or device-to-device transfers. Those services are managed by your platform provider under its policies; the developer does not receive those backups. Review your device backup settings to control them.

Google Play, your operating system and your device manufacturer may independently process information when distributing the app, managing backups or providing their own diagnostics. Their privacy policies govern those activities.

## Notifications

Reminders are optional. If you enable them and grant notification permission, the app schedules local notifications on your device. It does not register for remote push notifications or send your reminder settings to a notification server. You can disable reminders in the app or revoke notification permission in device settings. Depending on your device settings, reminder content may appear on the lock screen.

## Retention and deletion

Your records remain in the app's local storage until you edit or delete supported records, clear the app's storage through device settings, or uninstall the app. Disabling tracking or reminders retains existing history; it does not delete it. Some historical food entries retain saved nutrition and names even after the corresponding library item is changed or deleted.

On Android, clearing the app's storage removes its local database. Uninstalling normally removes local app data, but system backups may remain and may restore data when the app is reinstalled. Manage or delete those backups through your device or backup provider's controls. We cannot remotely retrieve or delete records stored only on your device or in your platform-managed backups.

## Security

App records are kept in the app's private storage and protected by the operating system's app isolation. The app does not provide its own database encryption or app-specific access lock. Protect your device with a screen lock and keep your operating system updated. No storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

## Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to include. We use that information to respond and resolve your request, retain it only as needed for that purpose or applicable obligations, and do not use it for advertising. Your email provider and ours process correspondence under their own policies. Please avoid sending sensitive health records unless necessary for your request.

## Children

The app is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through a developer-operated service. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us by email, contact us so we can address it.

## Health information

The app is a personal tracking tool, not a medical device. Nutrition and calorie calculations are estimates and are not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for advice about diet, weight management or fasting.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The revised policy will show an updated date. Material changes requiring notice or consent will be communicated as applicable.
